TIL: Curious Facts

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

The short version: TIL works without an account. If you create one, we store your email address and the facts you've saved or read, so they follow you between devices. That's all. We don't sell your data, we don't run ads, and we don't track you.

What we collect

Only what the app needs to do its job.

  • Without an account: nothing leaves your device. Your saved facts and reading progress are stored locally and never sent to us.
  • With an account: your email address, an encrypted version of your password, and your app data — the facts you've saved, the facts you've already seen, and which fact you were shown today.

We don't collect your name, phone number, location, contacts, photos, payment details, or advertising identifiers. The app has no analytics or tracking software in it.

Why we collect it

Your email and password exist so you can sign in. Your saved facts and reading progress are stored so that signing in on a second device shows you the same library and the same progress. We don't use your information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond running the app.

Who else sees it

We don't sell, rent, or share your personal information with anyone for their own use.

Account data is stored with Supabase, the hosting provider that runs the app's database and sign-in system on our behalf. They process it only to provide that service. We may disclose information if we're legally required to, such as in response to a valid court order.

How it's protected

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, so they can't be read — not by us, not by anyone with database access. Each account's data is locked to that account at the database level, so no user can read another user's saved facts.

No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security. Please use a password you don't reuse elsewhere.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app at any time: open the Account tab and choose Delete my account. This permanently removes your login and everything stored with it, on every device. It happens immediately and can't be undone.

If you'd rather we did it for you, email the address below and we'll delete your account within 30 days.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept for as long as your account exists. Once you delete your account, it's removed from our active database right away and clears from routine backups shortly after.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or ask for a copy of it. Email us and we'll take care of it. We won't treat you differently for asking.

Children

TIL isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we'll remove it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be noted in the app as well.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about your data, go to dolair02@gmail.com.
